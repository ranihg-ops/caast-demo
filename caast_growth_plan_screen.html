The Shopify edge - ads that know your store
Most tools generate ads blind to what's selling or in stock. Caast reads Hotcrown's live Shopify data:
Inventory Catalog ads sync to live stock - never a sold-out piece. Spend shifts to high-runners, and away from SKUs running low.
Bundles Recommends bundles to lift order value - you approve, we build the ads.
Discounts Time-boxed promo codes per campaign ("STARS15") - urgency for the buyer, clean attribution for you.
Pricing Surfaces signals - an underpriced high-demand piece, an anchor opportunity. Recommendations only.
Bundles, discounts, and pricing always come to you for approval first.
Learning firstNo verdicts in the first 7 days of a new campaign - Meta's learning phase is respected, not overridden.
Kill rulePause any ad whose CPA exceeds 150% of target, or whose CTR sits below 1% after enough impressions to judge.
Scale ruleWinners get +20-30% budget every 3-4 days while ROAS holds at or above target - never more. This is the path from $3,000 to $6,000, and it stops at your cap.
Creative refresh15-20+ new ad variations tested monthly - testing velocity is the strongest performance driver, and volume is what Caast is built for. Ads are replaced as frequency nears ~2.5-3.0, before fatigue reaches your CPA.
